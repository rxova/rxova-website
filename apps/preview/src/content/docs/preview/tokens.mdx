<p style={{ fontFamily: 'var(--rx-font-mono)', fontWeight: 500 }}>IBM Plex Mono 500 — emphasis</p>

Clicking between pages should never change the shape of this text after it paints.
That is `font-display: optional` doing its job; anything else is a regression.

A new `exclude` setting leaves other tools' evidence directories out of the patch, such as
`.basting` or `.saidso`. They are treated the way `.overlock` already was. Without it, those
directories changed the patch fingerprint on every turn and grew captured diffs recursively.

Entries are literal paths from the repository root. Globs, pathspec magic, `..` and filesystem paths
are refused, so the setting can only ever narrow what overlock looks at.

Four surfaces on one origin only feel like one site if they share their tokens. `@rxova/brand`
holds the colours, type and spacing, a Starlight theme built on them, and the header, footer
and project switcher every surface mounts.

Social cards are generated from the same tokens rather than drawn by hand, so a palette
change reaches the previews too.
